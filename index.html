ABOUT NIMHARA

Bringing the beauty of nature closer to you.

Nimhara Agriflora Pvt Ltd is a Sri Lankan flower and agricultural business dedicated to providing beautiful, fresh and quality flowers and plants.

From the cool highlands of Welimada and Badulla, we carefully cultivate and source flowers and plants for homes, florists, events, businesses and wholesale customers.
